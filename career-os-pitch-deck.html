05 / 12
AI Craft

Four AI systems working together.

🎯 E-01 Matching Algorithm

Every job match is scored: 70% skills overlap + 30% goal alignment. A candidate sees exactly which skills match, which are gaps, and a combined score. Not a black box — fully explained.

🤖 Claude Haiku — AI Coach

Streaming conversational coach. Prompt-cached personality + dynamic profile context. Knows your actual job, salary, skills, and goals — gives APAC-specific advice in MYR benchmarks.

🧭 Path Navigator

Claude generates 3 career navigation paths from your skill profile. True streaming — fast responses. Strong match (1–6 months), Emerging (6–18 months), Stretch goal (18–36 months) with realistic salary ranges.

📥 AI Profile Import

Upload resume PDF, paste LinkedIn text, or connect GitHub. Claude extracts skills, work history, and education — normalises and cross-references before adding to vault.
