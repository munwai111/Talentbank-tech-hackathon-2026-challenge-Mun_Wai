# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Fresh graduates and early-career professionals across Malaysia and Singapore —
digitally native, ambitious, and frustrated by credential-based hiring that
filters them out before anyone sees what they can actually do. They arrive
skeptical (burned by phantom jobs, opaque ATS filters, and a feed of rejection)
and need to feel, within seconds, that this tool sees *them* — their real
skills and trajectory, not their school name. Many are TVET/diploma holders and
career-pivoters the LinkedIn-native world overlooks.

## Product Purpose

Y.O.U (Your Odyssey Vector) is a skills-first career marketplace. It reads a
person's real skills — from GitHub, CV, work history — and plots the realistic
routes only they could take, in navigation language, with honest MYR salary
data. It supersedes credential filtering with demonstrated capability and
trajectory. The landing page is a marketing surface: its job is to convert a
skeptical graduate into a believer in the first scroll, and to make judges and
employers feel the product is real, precise, and alive.

## Brand Personality

Defiant, directional, precision-engineered, APAC-native, anti-corporate. The
voice is a sharp friend who knows the market cold — direct, honest above
comfortable, zero corporate jargon. Confident enough to show the actual product
output instead of describing it. Engineering credibility over marketing gloss.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS purple-to-blue gradients
- Inter-on-white (the default AI typeface and palette)
- Rounded card tiles with an icon above every heading
- Glassmorphism used decoratively
- Anything that reads as LinkedIn or a standard job board
- Cream/sand/parchment warm-neutral backgrounds
- Tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows and 01/02/03 numbered scaffolding on every section

## Design Principles

- **Show the output, don't describe it.** The hero carries the live Path
  Navigator data-viz — the actual product, not a screenshot or a promise.
- **Navigation, not prediction.** "Professionals like you typically…" never
  "you will…". Ranges, not single numbers. No false certainty.
- **Precision-instrument credibility.** Mono labels, tabular figures, system
  readouts. The interface should feel engineered, not marketed.
- **The candidate is the centre.** The brand mark literalises it — the O (YOU)
  is the still centre; the odyssey orbits, the vector launches from it.
- **APAC-native, not Western-translated.** MYR throughout, 12 languages, local
  market truth — built for this audience first, not localised after.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target: body text ≥4.5:1, large/UI text ≥3:1 — verified on the dark
canvas (chartreuse and violet label tints checked against #0A0A0F / card
surfaces). Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback that renders
the final state with no motion. Colour is never the sole carrier of meaning
(badges + labels accompany the route colour-coding). 12-language i18n for an
audience that is not English-first.
